At SRS Diamonds, we believe how a diamond is sourced matters as much as its quality. We are committed to responsible practices across our entire supply chain.

Certified Member

SRS Diamonds is a certified member of the Responsible Jewellery Council, the leading standard-setting organisation for the jewellery and watch industry. Our membership reflects our commitment to ethical, human rights, social and environmental practices.
